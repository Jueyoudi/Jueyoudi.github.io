## Research Interests

My primary research interest lies in **optimal control of dynamical systems**, with a particular focus on ensemble control and numerical methods for high-dimensional control problems.

I am interested in developing mathematical and computational methods for understanding and controlling parameter-dependent dynamical systems.

My research interests include:

- **Optimal Control and Dynamical Systems**
- **Ensemble Control**
- **Numerical Optimization and Computational Methods**
- **Moment-Based Methods and Constrained Control**

## Previous Research

### 1. Ensemble Control via Moment-Based Methods

A central focus of my previous research has been the control of parameter-dependent dynamical systems using shared control inputs.

For an ensemble of dynamical systems,

$$
\dot{x}(t,\beta)
=
A(\beta)x(t,\beta)
+
B(\beta)u(t),
$$

where $\beta$ represents a system parameter, the objective is to steer the ensemble toward a desired terminal configuration.

I have explored moment-based representations and finite-dimensional approximations to formulate and numerically solve these control problems.

My numerical experiments include:

- Geometric pattern transformations
- Distribution steering
- Terminal state matching
- Control energy minimization

### 2. State-Constrained Ensemble Optimal Control

I have also investigated approaches for incorporating state constraints into ensemble optimal control.

My work has explored integral constraints and positive semidefinite matrix conditions in moment-based formulations.

I am particularly interested in understanding how finite-dimensional approximations affect feasibility and constraint satisfaction.

### 3. Computational Mechanics and Modal Analysis

During my research experience at Texas A&M University, I worked on computational simulations involving structural dynamics and modal analysis.

I gained experience with LS-DYNA, finite element simulation, modal decomposition, and numerical processing of simulation data.

This experience strengthened my interest in the interaction between mathematical models and computational methods.

## Future Research Directions

For my doctoral studies, I hope to further explore optimal control theory and computational methods for complex dynamical systems.

In particular, I am interested in:

- Optimal control of high-dimensional and parameter-dependent systems
- Numerical methods for constrained optimal control
- Computationally efficient approximations of dynamical systems
- Connections between control theory, optimization, and real-world dynamical systems

I am especially interested in research that combines mathematical analysis with numerical implementation and simulation.
